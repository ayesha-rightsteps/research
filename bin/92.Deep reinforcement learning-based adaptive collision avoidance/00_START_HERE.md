# Hey Ayesha — Read This First

## What is this paper about?

This paper tackles a critical problem in modern military aviation: how can a UAV (unmanned aerial vehicle) automatically avoid collisions with other aircraft — both manned jets and other drones — when flying in a chaotic battlefield where it cannot see the full picture and wind is constantly changing its path? The researchers propose a smart AI system called HPER-D3QN that teaches the UAV through trial-and-error learning (deep reinforcement learning) to dodge threats intelligently by prioritizing the most dangerous ones first. The result is a UAV that achieves a 96.28% mission success rate even in extremely crowded airspace with 25 aircraft, outperforming every competing method tested.

## Why does it matter?

Modern warfare increasingly relies on UAVs flying alongside manned aircraft, but collisions in that shared airspace are a real and deadly risk — especially when communication systems fail and the UAV cannot know what other aircraft are doing. A robust autonomous collision avoidance system like the one in this paper is foundational technology for making joint human-drone military operations safe and effective in real combat scenarios.
