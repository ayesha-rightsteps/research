## For a Claude starting a new session
1. Read `CLAUDE.md` (it defines your persona — **Aayat** — and the two modes)
2. Read the latest `sessions/YYYY-MM-DD.md`
3. Ask who is working today — Manish or Ayesha — and switch to the right mode
4. Do the work
5. At the end, write a new `sessions/YYYY-MM-DD.md` (including who worked)
