Summary

What has been done since 12 October

  • The four changes proposed on 12 October were implemented (separate mission and safety value heads, obstacle danger in PAH's τ input, 24-ray LiDAR sensing, sized obstacle discs). PAH keeps the synopsis inputs (τ, d_target, n_conflict).
  • The first trained PAH showed a measurable bias against safety. The cause was found, PAH's learning rule was changed once, and the design was then frozen.
  • Five of our methods and five base-paper methods were trained in the same environment with the same settings: 10 seeds each, 186 trained models in total.
  • Primary result: MAPPO + PAH is better than Fixed-weight MAPPO with 8 drones, +8.6 points [95% CI +1.7, +15.4]. It ranks first at both stages and is above every base-paper method.
Primary question · pre-registered before training

Stage 3 (8 drones, 20 obstacles): MAPPO + PAH − Fixed-weight MAPPO, mission success

+8.6 points 95% Welch CI [+1.7, +15.4] · 10 vs 10 seeds

The interval is entirely above zero, so by the rule fixed before training MAPPO + PAH is better. The gain is plausibly between about 2 and 15 points. MAPPO + PAH also finishes fastest (61 vs 76 steps on average in successful episodes).

1 · Design changes

From v3 to v4

The 12 October diagnostics showed that failures were obstacle collisions, PAH could not see obstacles, and α barely changed the learning signal. A check on the laptop confirmed the last point: in the v3 design Plain MAPPO (89.9%) and Fixed-weight MAPPO (88.2%) were indistinguishable, CI [−1.1, +4.5]. The redesign:

Change (proposed 12 Oct)What was implemented
1. Separate value headsThe critic has a mission head and a safety head; α mixes the two advantages for the actor.
2. Obstacle danger for PAHτ is now the time to the nearest drone or obstacle collision (capped at 3 steps). The input set stays τ, d_target, n_conflict.
3. LiDAR-like sensing24 rays, 15° apart, range 100 (v3: four axis-aligned distances).
4. Sized, denser obstaclesDiscs of radius 12: 10 at Stage 2, 20 at Stage 3.

Two further changes came from sanity runs on Plain MAPPO only (seeds 101/102, separate from the main seeds), and were then frozen for every method:

  • Environment bug fixed: targets bounced off obstacles too close to them, so a drone hovering on its target was dragged into the disc. A hand-written LiDAR rule then reached 82% success with no obstacle collisions, which shows the task is solvable.
  • Density and safety reward: with 20/40 discs Plain MAPPO learned too slowly, so 10/20 were used (the denser setting is kept for the density sweep). Safety shaping starts 20 units from a hazard, and a collision costs −10.
2 · PAH

The first PAH was biased; the fixed PAH learns risk

The first v4 PAH learned α through the actor's PPO objective. The gradient of α·Amission + (1−α)·Asafety with respect to α is Amission − Asafety, and in danger Asafety is strongly negative, so PAH learned to push α towards the mission exactly when danger was close (measured correction +0.200, its maximum, when τ < 1). This was already visible in v3 and is reported as a finding.

The fixed PAH has the same three inputs and the same 3→32→1 network. It predicts the risk p of the coming safety cost by regression on the observed safety return, and sets α = 0.9 − 0.8·p. No gradient reaches PAH from the actor loss. The change was made once and tested on synthetic data and a 300-episode smoke run before the main training.

α weights the mission; 1 − α weights safety. PAH turns towards safety earlier than the hand-coded rule (0.32 vs 0.51 at 1 ≤ τ < 2). 80% of its danger signals come from obstacles.

3 · Protocol

How the results were produced

  • Same environment, training budget (8,000 episodes) and hyperparameters for all methods. Tuning was done once, on Plain MAPPO only, and frozen before any other method was trained.
  • 10 training seeds (42–51) per method and stage. Final test: 200 episodes per model, deterministic actions, evaluation seed 7001, so every model sees the same 200 layouts.
  • Success = every drone reaches its assigned target with no collision within 500 steps (as in DA-MAPPO).
  • Reading rule, fixed in advance: difference of mean success with a 95% Welch CI. Only the Stage 3 comparison above is primary; every other comparison is secondary, with no multiple-testing correction.

6 · Checks

Robustness and reproducibility

  • Seed 42 left out. Because the first PAH's bias was seen on seed 42, the main comparisons were recomputed without it: Stage 3 PAH − Fixed-weight +7.9 [+0.7, +15.1], PAH − τ-rule +12.8 [+5.9, +19.6]; Stage 2 PAH − Fixed-weight +7.7 [+3.1, +12.2]. On seed 42 itself the fixed PAH is below the τ-rule (79.5% vs 82.0%).
  • Re-run. All 186 final evaluations were run again on 16 October and gave byte-identical results; all tables are generated by script from the raw files.
  • Tests. Environment, agent and baseline unit tests pass, including a test that the success definition is identical for every assignment mode.
7 · Limits

What the thesis will state

  • The primary CI is wide; its lower end is +1.7 points.
  • At Stage 2, MAPPO + PAH vs the hand-coded τ-rule is not settled: +2.0 [−2.6, +6.6]. The learned α is clearly better only with 8 drones.
  • Base-paper methods use our shared settings, tuned on Plain MAPPO; they were not tuned individually, and their absolute scores are below the papers' 3D results.
  • RMAPPO did not learn under the shared settings (exploration collapsed early; 0% on 6 seeds at Stage 3; no implementation bug found). It is reported in an appendix with the reason, not as a performance number.
  • One 2D simulated environment; no 3D or hardware claims.
