## 15. Stage 3 Hungarian ablation — result (Ayesha ne Kaggle pe chalaya)

**Who:** Ayesha
**Notebook:** `code/notebooks/v3/stage3/ablation-hungarian/kaggle_stage3_hungarian_ablation.ipynb`
**Seed:** 42 · **Output:** `code/notebooks/v3/stage3/ablation-hungarian/output/`

### Result

| Method | Success | Collision | Notes |
|--------|---------|-----------|-------|
| M — Hungarian ON (seed 42) | 74.8% | 25.2% | Stage 3 M baseline |
| B4 — Hungarian ON (seed 42) | 74.7% | 25.3% | Stage 3 B4 baseline |
| **M — Hungarian OFF (seed 42)** | **54.2%** | **45.8%** | ye ablation |

*(54.2% = last 20 training-time eval points ka mean, 20-episode eval batches —
noisy, lekin 20-point gap itna bada hai ki direction clear hai. Proper 200-episode
final eval se number thoda alag ho sakta hai.)*

### Key findings

1. **Assignment contribution ~20 percentage points** — Hungarian hataane se
   success 74.8% → 54.2%, collision 25.2% → 45.8%. Ye Stage 3 (8 drones) mein
   assignment ka major role confirm karta hai.
2. **α-weighting contribution ~0.1%** — M vs B4 ka fark (74.8% vs 74.7%) —
   null result, same as pehle.
3. **PAH direction-check preserved even without Hungarian:** danger mein α ≈ 13%
   (safety-first ✓), safe mein α ≈ 83% (mission-first ✓). Mechanism sahi kaam
   karta hai, performance assignment ki wajah se girti hai, PAH ki wajah se nahi.
4. **DA-MAPPO se align karta hai:** unka ablation bhi assignment hataane pe
   dramatic collapse dikhata hai (`07_literature_comparison.md` Section 3).

### Interpretation

Assignment component (~20 pts) >> α-weighting component (~0.1 pts). Thesis ka
main contribution (PAH) mechanism level pe sahi hai (direction-check preserved),
lekin outcome level pe assignment ki importance assignment component ko dominate
karne nahi deta. Honest reporting: assignment critical hai, learned α marginal hai.
