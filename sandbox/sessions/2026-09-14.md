## B. SABSE BADI PROBLEM — PAH ka "fix" ek downgrade hai, fix nahi

Purana bug: PAH khud apna reward maximize kar raha tha (`01_pah_design.md` mein
jo "reward hacking" warn kiya tha, wahi). Naya "fix" (`mappo.py`):

```python
α_target = α_min + (α_max−α_min) · τ_norm · (1 − 0.3·n_norm)
pah_loss = MSE(α, α_target)
```

**Yeh reinforcement learning hai hi nahi.** PAH ab bas ek **haath se likhe
formula ko copy karna** seekh raha hai — reward/outcome se kuch seekh nahi
raha. `01_pah_design.md` mein humne isi ko khud "Option C" bola tha aur likha
tha: *"Hacking nahi, par 'learned' wala novelty kamzor. Ablation ke liye achha
[main method ke liye nahi]."*

Matlab: **thesis ka poora point ("hum α ko experience se seekhte hain, hand-tune
nahi karte") ab jhoot ho gaya** — α ab bhi hand-tuned hai, bas neural network ke
through indirect tareeqe se. Jab final comparison hoga "PAH vs fixed-α", PAH ka
output basically usi formula jaisa hoga jo humne khud likha — koi bhi examiner
poochh sakta hai: *"iske seekhe hue alpha aur aapke formula mein farak kya hai?"*
