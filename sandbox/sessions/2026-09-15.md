## 4. Design decisions

- **Option B "lite" chosen for PAH:** Single shared critic baseline for both `A_mission` and `A_safety` (not two critic heads). Reason: avoids adding parameters to critic (closer to synopsis), sufficient for thesis scope. If training is unstable, two-head extension is the clear next step.
- **target_radius=25m for training:** 5% of world_size=500. Same proportional catchment as original 100m world. Can be tightened in later stages as policy matures.
- **success_bonus=20:** Strong enough to dominate the step-level r_mission signal (~-0.25/step average). Placed inside `step()` so GAE sees it as part of the trajectory naturally.
- **eval_env success_bonus=0:** Evaluation should measure true task completion, not training-shaped reward.
- **Option C becomes ablation:** Hand-crafted `α_target = f(τ,n)` regression is a valid thesis comparison figure ("learned PAH vs heuristic PAH") — documented in `01_pah_design.md` Section 9.

     100 |     0.0% |       0.0% |  -0.0086 |   3.5828 |   3.3m

**Diagnosis:**
- Entropy **rising** (3.58 → 3.81) — policy zyada random ho rahi thi, seekh nahi rahi
- Actor loss ~0.003-0.009 — almost zero, koi meaningful gradient nahi
- Root cause 1: `ent_coef=0.02` bahut zyada — entropy bonus (0.02×3.6=0.072) actor loss (0.003-0.009) se **8-24x bada** tha. Optimizer mostly entropy maximize kar raha tha
- Root cause 2: Reward sirf negative (`r_mission = -dist/500`) — policy ko koi positive signal nahi milta ki "yeh direction sahi hai"

## v4 fixes — v3 notebook in-place update kiya

| Fix | v3 | v4 |
|-----|----|----|
| `ent_coef` | 0.02 (too high) | **0.003** |
| Reward formula | `0.5*r_mission + 0.5*r_safety` | **`0.4*r_progress + 0.3*r_mission + 0.3*r_safety`** |
| `r_progress` | missing | **`dot(vel, dir_to_target) / max_speed` ∈ [-1,+1]** |

`r_progress` explanation: har step pe drone ka velocity target ki direction mein kitna hai — target ki taraf jaao = positive reward, ulti taraf = negative. Yeh woh dense signal hai jo policy ko "seedha chalo" sikhata hai.
