## 4. Design decisions

- **Option B "lite" chosen for PAH:** Single shared critic baseline for both `A_mission` and `A_safety` (not two critic heads). Reason: avoids adding parameters to critic (closer to synopsis), sufficient for thesis scope. If training is unstable, two-head extension is the clear next step.
- **target_radius=25m for training:** 5% of world_size=500. Same proportional catchment as original 100m world. Can be tightened in later stages as policy matures.
- **success_bonus=20:** Strong enough to dominate the step-level r_mission signal (~-0.25/step average). Placed inside `step()` so GAE sees it as part of the trajectory naturally.
- **eval_env success_bonus=0:** Evaluation should measure true task completion, not training-shaped reward.
- **Option C becomes ablation:** Hand-crafted `α_target = f(τ,n)` regression is a valid thesis comparison figure ("learned PAH vs heuristic PAH") — documented in `01_pah_design.md` Section 9.

## Manish's review — no bugs, but two things to know

**No red flags in the numbers themselves:**
- **Negative entropy is normal, not a bug.** Continuous (Gaussian) action-space
  entropy is *differential* entropy — unlike discrete entropy it has no floor
  at 0. Once the policy's action std shrinks below ~0.24, entropy goes
  negative. This just means the policy became confident/near-deterministic —
  expected as training converges, not an error.
- **100% success from ep100, 0% collision throughout** is plausible, not
  suspicious, given this config: `target_radius=25m` (generous catchment),
  `max_steps=300` at `max_speed=5` gives a huge time budget (1500 units vs a
  ~707m worst-case diagonal), zero obstacles, only 3 drones in a big empty
  500×500 world. This task is genuinely easy under this configuration.
- Tiny/sign-flipping actor-loss is consistent with a policy that's already
  near-optimal for this task — little left to improve, so little loss signal.
  Not a bug.

**New concern raised by this specific result — task may be too easy to prove
the point.** Hitting 100% success this fast, in an obstacle-free world with a
generous target radius and a huge time budget, does not by itself show that
the **assignment mechanism** (Hungarian-augmented observation) is doing

at all might solve this exact config too. Recommended before moving to
Stage 2: **run the ablation from `03_baseline_specs.md`** — retrain (or
re-evaluate) with the Hungarian-assignment observation removed. If success
