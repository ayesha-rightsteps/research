(danger mein weight ~1.0, safe mein ~0.1). `prior_coef` bhi `0.05 → 0.15`
badhaya.

## 4. Design decisions

**Kyun weighted prior, coefficient sirf badhane ki jagah:** Pehla fix (coefficient
0.01→0.05, target flat 0.5→τ-informed) fail hua kyunki root cause sirf "coefficient
kam tha" nahi tha — root cause ye tha ki rare danger-samples ek uniform-averaged
loss mein dab jaate hain jab wo poore batch ka chhota fraction hon. Sirf
coefficient badhana (0.05→0.5 jaisa) safe samples ko bhi utna hi zyada affect
karta, jo zaroori nahi tha (safe-alpha values already reasonable the). Weighting
zyada targeted hai — sirf danger samples pe zyada zor, safe samples pe halka.
