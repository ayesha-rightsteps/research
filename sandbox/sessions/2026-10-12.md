### 10.1 Sawal: "Hum graph attention mechanism kyun use nahi kar rahe?"

**Graph attention kya karta hai (IGAT-MARL):** (1) conflict graph batata hai kaun se neighbours
dekhne hain; (2) attention khud seekhti hai ke har neighbour ko kitna weight dena hai.

**Hum kya karte hain:** IGAT ka pehla hissa liya, doosra nahi.
- ✅ Conflict graph (DCPA/TCPA) — IGAT-MARL se
- ❌ Attention nahi — uski jagah conflict neighbours ko **time-to-collision ke hisaab se sort**
  karke pehle 4 (K_nbr=4), zero-padding + mask, simple MLP ko. Yani attention ka kaam ek haath
  ke rule se ("jo pehle takrayega woh pehle") — network khud weight nahi seekhta.

**Faisla kyun hua (docs mein likha hai):** `00_problem_formalization.md` — "We start with
padding+mask **for simplicity**; the graph-attention encoder is available for the IGAT-style
baseline (B3)." `03_baseline_specs.md` — "GAT depth (2×2): optional; we may start with a lighter
neighbor encoder and only use GAT for the B3 baseline." Wajahein: asaani (MLP ko fixed input);
thesis ka contribution PAH hai (reward weighting), encoder nahi; GAT B3 ke liye rakha tha —
**lekin B3 kabhi bana hi nahi.**

**Imaandar jawab:**
- Thesis claim (PAH) ke liye seedha masla nahi — PAH graph attention pe depend nahi karta.

### 13.2 Galti: D2 interpretation overreach kar gaya

(caveat "lower bound" ke saath). Training code (`stage3/var-alpha` Cell 4, `RolloutBuffer`) dekha:

```
delta_m = r_mission + γ·V(s') − V(s)
delta_s = r_safety  + γ·V(s') − V(s)      # V = SINGLE critic, trained on the COMBINED (α-weighted) reward
adv_m, adv_s = GAE(delta_m), GAE(delta_s)
adv_m_flat = z-normalize(adv_m);  adv_s_flat = z-normalize(adv_s)   # separately
w_adv = α·adv_m_flat + (1−α)·adv_s_flat
```

Consequences:
1. **`adv_s` ≠ 0 almost every step**, even when `r_safety = 0` — it carries the combined critic's TD
   term. So "α matters only ~1% of the time" is **wrong as stated**. D2's measured numbers (how often
   `r_safety ≠ 0`) are correct; the conclusion drawn from them was not.
2. **Alternative explanation for the M ≈ H ≈ B4 tie (HYPOTHESIS):** `adv_m` and `adv_s` share the same
   value-TD backbone and differ only by `r_mission` vs `r_safety`; after separate z-normalization they may
   be highly correlated, so re-weighting them with α changes the policy gradient little. Needs measurement.
3. **Design question for v3:** the "safety advantage" is not a pure safety signal — it is computed
   against a baseline of the combined return. Worth discussing before any v4 design.
